Go-To-Market Strategy

Trust-led, CSR-funded entry through NGO networks → conversion to paid retail demand as own-manufacturing scales.

Core Thesis

Cater to the nutrition gap among pregnant & lactating women and infant & young children. Leverage CSR-funded free distribution through NGO networks to build trust across rural India, and become a trusted choice in urban households through online and offline channels — delivering the right nutrition at the right time while creating measurable social impact at scale.
